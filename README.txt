PAGETURN STORYBOOK WEBSITE

HOW TO OPEN
1. Open the wattpad_storybook folder.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome or another browser.

PROJECT FEATURES
- 30 uploaded book covers linked to their matching titles, with original single-chapter sample stories.
- Search by title, series, genre, or synopsis.
- Filter stories by genre.
- Book details include the uploaded cover, author field, genre, synopsis, and one chapter.
- Responsive layout, working page navigation, and dark mode.
- Wattpad buttons open a search for the corresponding title.

PERSONALIZE
- In script.js, replace the authorName value 'Your Wattpad Name' with the author's preferred name.
- The title list had 29 entries, while 30 cover images were uploaded. The 30th cover reads 'A Perfect Conquest', so that title is included as the 30th story.
- The single-chapter stories are original sample content inspired by the titles, not copied Wattpad chapters.
- The uploaded JPG cover filenames are linked to their corresponding stories in script.js.

FILES
- index.html: semantic page and story dialog structure
- style.css: responsive design, cover styles, and dark theme
- script.js: story data, search, genre filter, details dialog, Wattpad links, and dark mode
- images/: uploaded story covers, SVG artwork, and the project requirements image

NOTES
- The website works locally. An internet connection is needed only for Google Fonts and Wattpad search links.
